Privacy and data

Schola Clash stores your sign-in email, display name, class membership, chapter content, practice results, matches and ratings. Your teacher can see the progress of students in their teaching space. Students can see classmates' names and shared match results.

Local drafts

Unsaved chapter drafts expire after 7 days. Expired drafts are removed when you next open the chapter editor; signing out also removes your drafts. Unsubmitted ranked answers stay on this device until submission, sign-out or expiry. Local drafts do not sync to another device.

Error reports

Signed-in error reports contain your account ID, the operation category and an error code. They do not contain passwords, answer text, emails, URL tokens or stack traces. Reports are retained for 30 days.

Retention and deletion

Practice results are retained for up to 365 days. Resolved deletion requests and cancelled or rejected requests are retained for up to 90 days. These periods depend on the administrator running the scheduled maintenance job.

Archived classes and chapters retain membership and historical results. Match records and ratings remain until the school administrator removes or anonymizes them under the school's retention rules.

To request deletion, open My account → Request account deletion. You can track or cancel a pending request there. The administrator reviews the request and any record retention requirements. Submitting a request does not immediately erase your account. Contact your teacher or school administrator for access, correction, or deletion requests.

Backups

Administrators must store backups encrypted and restrict access to them. A deletion completed in the application may remain in an older backup until that backup expires. The backup configuration and school retention policy must be set before school use.
